Modifications by the Fruit Motion project: exact trims of these existing sounds, high/low-pass filtering, 4 ms/25 ms edge fades, conservative layer mixes, mild dynamic leveling on the fruit and obstacle cues, peak normalization to −9…−15 dBFS, and 24 kHz mono 16-bit PCM WAV encoding. Each bank has ten different fruit-specific recordings or explicitly identified material proxies; toy layers are additional actual authored sounds, not ten slight pitch shifts of a generic cue. The obstacle uses a processed microphone tap, a tiny leather-chair tap, jaw harp and licensed cartoon accents, not a real explosion. Confirmations on every third combo are independent reward-only recordings played after the actual fruit cue; no repeated or generic substitute cut. No browser-generated oscillator or synthesized primary sound-effect replacement is used.

`scripts/prepare-sfx.mjs` retains the original pins, verifies each new public author download and exact archive member by SHA-256, and checks every prepared WAV against the independently frozen B-R3 selection in `scripts/sfx-v16-selection.json`. `assets/sfx/build-manifest.json` records source title, license, original attachment, checksum, layer edits, output checksum and waveform measurements for all 32 clips. The manifest is retained in the deployed static site. `docs/benchmarks/v1.6/audio/` contains the actual short CC0 reference WAV archive, a reproducible A/B/C comparison, explicit material/attribution provenance and the final selected previews. The eight non-fruit control WAVs and five original music files remain byte-identical. Only prepared same-origin short WAVs are decoded during play; original archives and third-party download addresses are build-time provenance, never runtime network dependencies.
